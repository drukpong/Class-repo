About us

Birchwood started as a cart outside the train station with one grinder and a bad extension cord. Fifteen years later we're still doing the same thing, just with better equipment and a roof.

We buy green coffee directly from six farms, roast it ourselves in the back room, and pull shots on a machine that gets serviced more often than my car. If a batch doesn't taste right, it doesn't go on the shelf.
